I built **SalatTime**, a Garmin widget that brings accurate Islamic prayer times to your wrist. As someone who wears a Garmin watch daily, I wanted a native way to check prayer times without pulling out my phone.

## Features

SalatTime displays all six daily prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Duhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha) directly on your Garmin watch:

- **Dual Calendar Display**: Shows both Gregorian and Hijri (Islamic) dates
- **Moon Phase Visualization**: Current moon phase with illumination percentage
- **Sun Position Chart**: A visual sun path showing sunrise, sunset, and current position
- **Widget Glance**: Quick view showing the next prayer with a countdown and a visual day/night cycle bar

## Calculation Methods

SalatTime supports 12 calculation methods to match your region:

- Muslim World League
- Egyptian General Authority
- University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi
- Umm Al-Qura (Makkah)
- Dubai
- Moon Sighting Committee
- ISNA (North America)
- Kuwait
- Qatar
- Singapore
- Diyanet (Turkey)

You can also choose between Standard and Hanafi Asr calculation, plus high latitude adjustments for locations like Northern Europe.

## The Glance View

The widget glance shows at a glance:

- Next prayer name and countdown
- A 24-hour day/night cycle bar
- Markers for each prayer time (green = upcoming, gray = past)
- Current time indicator
